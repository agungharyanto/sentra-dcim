# Getting started

## Signing in

Sign in with your **personal account** (username or NIP and password) on the **Sign in** page. Accounts are created
by an administrator; the first password must be changed. Every sign-in also asks for a 6-digit code from the
authenticator app on your phone ([Two-step verification](../auth/mfa.md)). Full steps:
[Signing in and out](../auth/login.md).

After signing in you land on the [Dashboard](../dashboard/index.md). Your name is shown in the top right; from there
you can open [My sessions](../auth/sesi.md), [change your password](../auth/ganti-password.md) or **Sign out**.

## Navigation

- The **left sidebar** holds every menu, grouped into Monitoring, Infrastructure, Operations and System.
- Menus marked **Coming soon** are not usable yet; opening one shows a short description of that module.
- The button at the bottom left **collapses the sidebar** to icons only; hover or focus an icon to see its name.
  The browser remembers this choice.
- On a phone or tablet, open the menu with the **☰** button at the top left, then close it with **Esc** or ×.
- Below the page title there is a **breadcrumb**, for example *Sentra DCIM › Monitoring › Dashboard*.

## Language, theme and clock

- **ID/EN** — switches the display language. The choice is stored in this browser.
- **Dark / Light / Follow system** — switches the theme. Dark is the default.
- The **clock** in the header uses the time zone configured for Sentra DCIM (WIB by default), not your device's
  time zone. All data is still stored in UTC.

## Keyboard

- Press **Tab** once after a page opens to reveal the **Skip to content** link.
- Every menu, button and dialog works with **Tab**, **Enter** and **Esc**.
